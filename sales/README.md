# Sales collateral

`proposal-template.html` → `Proposal-Template.pdf` — a two-page proposal to send after a meeting.

## Filling it in

Every placeholder is **highlighted in amber** in both the HTML and the PDF, so nothing can be sent
half-finished by accident. Open the HTML in any editor and search for `class="fill"` — there are 19.

The ones that matter most:

| Placeholder | What to put |
|---|---|
| The leak paragraph | **The real timestamp** from filling in their own ad, and the real gap before anyone called. This is the whole pitch — do not fake it. |
| Project Name | Their actual project, the one you loaded into the Projects tab for the demo |
| Prices | Adjust per client. If nobody flinches, they are too low. |
| Refund threshold | The number of qualified conversations you will guarantee in 30 days |

## Re-rendering the PDF

Open `proposal-template.html` in Chrome → **Print** → **Save as PDF**, with:

- Paper size **A4**
- Margins **Default**
- **Background graphics ON** — without this the navy headers and coloured callouts print blank

It is laid out to land on exactly two pages. If you add a paragraph it will spill to three, so cut
something else to compensate.

## Before the meeting

1. Load their real project into the `Projects` tab — real photos, real price, real brochure
2. Check the WhatsApp 24-hour window is open on your demo phone, or the live demo fails in the room
3. Fill in their own enquiry form and note the timestamp

The demo is the proof. The proposal is just what they read afterwards.
